01 — POINT
The page is the document.
Pins attach to the element, not to a screenshot. Ship a change and the pin stays on the headline it was about.
02 — SAY IT PLAINLY
No tickets, no repro steps.
Write the sentence you would have said out loud. The file, the line, and the surrounding markup travel with it.
03 — IT COMES BACK CHANGED
A reply, and the work.
Your teammate answers in the thread with the change attached. Read it, apply it, keep going.
CONNECTED THREADS

The conversation doesn't move.

Link a pin to a channel and replies flow both directions. Whoever weighs in from Slack or WhatsApp lands in the thread, next to the change they were arguing about.
